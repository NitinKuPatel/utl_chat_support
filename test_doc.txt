
# Solar Inverter Product Manual (Mock)

## Model: UTL-Solar-X1

### Safety Instructions
Always disconnect the battery before maintenance.
Do not cover the cooling vents.

### Troubleshooting
**Error 01**: Low Battery Voltage. Recharge battery or check connections.
**Error 02**: Overload. Disconnect non-essential loads.
**Error 03**: Grid Freq High. Check grid settings.

### User Policy (Customer)
Warranty is valid for 2 years.
Returns accepted within 30 days.

### Internal Technical Note (Employee)
To reset the factory settings, hold the UP and DOWN buttons for 10 seconds.
This action is not visible to customers.
The debug pin is 1234.
